## Who I am

My name is Manuel Cencerrado. I'm studying Computer Engineering at Universidad Francisco de Vitoria, starting my third year this fall, specializing in **cybersecurity**.

I'm curious by nature, and that curiosity is exactly what pulled me into the world of cybersecurity and cloud: the more I learn, the more I want to keep pulling the thread. This blog is, at its core, a way of channeling that curiosity and keeping a record of what I discover along the way.

This blog comes from a very simple need: **I want to understand what I study better, and explaining it is the best way to really learn it**. If it happens to be useful to someone else too, even better.

Why **pcapcafe**? Because a `.pcap` is the format used to store network traffic captures (something you'll work with a lot in cybersecurity), and because this blog is a bit like those coffee-break chats: informal, curious, with no pretense of being the definitive source on anything — just sharing what I'm learning.

## What this blog will cover

I don't want this to be just another "10 cybersecurity tricks" blog. My idea is to comment on **current events**: hacks, leaks, vulnerabilities (CVEs), and news from the cloud world (AWS, Azure, GCP), explaining what happened, why it happened, and what we can learn from it.

Whenever I can, I'll try to connect that news with what I see at university, because I think that intersection of theory and current events is where you learn the most.

In short, here's what you'll find:

- **Analysis of security and cloud news**, explained clearly and without unnecessary jargon.
- **Technical concepts** (types of vulnerabilities, tools, best practices) explained through real, current cases.
- **Reflections on my own path**: what I'm studying, which certifications I'm working toward, and what I'd tell someone just starting out in this field.

## Why it might interest you

If you're a computer science student, if you're interested in cybersecurity or cloud, or if you simply enjoy understanding what's behind the latest hack in the news, this blog is for you. I don't claim to be the world's biggest expert — I'm just getting started — but that's exactly why I think I can explain things in an approachable way, without assuming you already know what's going on.

## What to expect

I'll publish a new article every **Monday**. I'd rather keep a steady, sustainable pace than promise several posts a week and fail to deliver. I'll also share the personal projects I put together along the way (labs, cloud deployments, tools) as I get them ready.

If you want to comment on something, correct me, or just chat about a topic, you can reach me on LinkedIn: @manuelcencerrado.

Thanks for stopping by pcapcafe, and welcome!
